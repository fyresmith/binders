# Manuscript (Word or PDF)

A manuscript is a Word file (.docx), or a PDF, in standard manuscript format, the form agents and editors ask for.
Choose **Manuscript** in the [Export window](export.md).

## What you get

- 12-point Times New Roman, double-spaced, with one-inch margins.
- A half-inch first-line indent on every paragraph.
- A header with your surname, the title and the page number.
- A title page with your name, your contact details and the word count, rounded.
- Each chapter on a new page, a third of the way down.
- `#` between scenes.

## Choices

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Style** | See the three styles below. The sliders button beside it is **Edit this style** |
| **File** | **Word (.docx)** or **PDF** |
| **Paper** | For a PDF: **Letter** or **A4** |
| **What goes out** | The whole book, a folder, a saved filter, or the selected cards: see [What goes out](export.md#what-goes-out). With part of the book, **Numbering** says whether chapters keep their numbers from the whole book or start from 1 |
| **Your name** | Asked for once, when neither the book nor Binders' settings has an author. It is kept in Binders' settings |

A dedication, acknowledgements and the like are left out of a manuscript as it comes. To take one, tick its box in
**Contents**.

### Styles

| Style | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Standard manuscript** | As described above |
| **Standard manuscript, Courier** | The same in Courier New, with italics underlined |
| **Plain, for a typesetter** | Single-spaced, no title page or header, chapters starting at the top of a page, `***` between scenes |
| **Short story** | No page for the title: your name, contact details and the word count at the top of the first page, the title a quarter of the way down it, "by" and your name under it, and the story beginning under that. No header on the first page; "Surname / TITLE / 2" from the second |

### A short story

Put a story's notes side by side in the binder, set **Structure** to **Folders are chapters, notes are scenes**, and
choose the **Short story** style. The notes are the scenes of one chapter, parted by the scene break, and a story of one
chapter has no "Chapter One" over it: the title is above. It is the same in the Word file and in the PDF. (A longer
piece with folders has its chapters headed as ever; the first follows the title on the first page.) The style puts
the title there with **Title page: On the first page of the text**, which any manuscript style can choose.

The style you choose is kept with the book, in its binder note. A book that hasn't chosen one uses the style last
used in the vault.

To change a style or make your own (the typeface, the line spacing, italics underlined, the typeface (including Courier Prime, or any font you name), what a chapter's heading says, where a chapter starts, the
scene break, what runs along the top, the title page), see [Styles](export-styles.md).

## As a PDF

Set **File** to **PDF** and the manuscript is made as a PDF on Letter or A4 paper, with the same title page, header
and spacing.

- The window shows the pages themselves, exactly as they will print, and the bar says how many there are.
- The header and its page number are on every page of the text. The title page has neither.
- The typeface is embedded in the PDF. A font of your own that this computer doesn't have is replaced by Courier or
  Times, and the window says so; see [A manuscript's typeface](export-styles.md#a-manuscripts-typeface).
- A PDF is made on a computer. On a phone or tablet you can look at the pages, and export it on a computer.
- **File** and **Paper** are kept in Binders' settings, for the vault.
- Exporting the PDF to the place the Word file went puts it beside the Word file.

## Your name and contact details

The title page takes your name and the lines under it (an address, an email, a phone number) from **Settings →
Binders → Export**: **Your name** and **Contact details**.

For one book with another author or title, set **Author** and **Title** in
[Book details](book-details.md). They are kept as `author` and `title` in the binder note.

## Real Word styles

The file is built the way Word expects, so it can be restyled in Word or brought into Vellum or Atticus:

- chapters are Heading 1;
- scene breaks have a style of their own;
- footnotes are Word footnotes;
- links are links.

## The preview

For a Word file the preview is the manuscript's text as it will read, on paper: the headings, the breaks and the
notes, with the header shown once. It is not the pages. Word sets its own lines and turns its own pages. For a PDF
the preview is the pages.

## How the book is put together

How folders and notes become chapters and scenes, and what your Markdown becomes, is the same for every book. See
[Book details and structure](book-details.md).

## Limits

- The title page's "by" and "about 80,000 words" are in English whatever the book's language: standard manuscript
  format is an English-language convention.
- Pictures are PNG, JPEG or GIF.
- The Word file has been opened in LibreOffice. It hasn't yet been opened in Word, Pages or Google Docs, or brought
  into Vellum or Atticus, by the maintainer. See [Known limitations](limitations.md#not-yet-tried).
