# Manuscript

The manuscript shows every note in the binder or folder, in order, as one page: folder names as headings, each
note's title above its text. It is the place to write and to read the book straight through.

![The manuscript](images/manuscript.png)

## Writing in it

Each section is a real Obsidian editor on that note. Typing, undo, links, formatting and Obsidian's commands work
as they do in the note itself. What you type goes into that note and is saved as Obsidian saves any note you edit.

Properties are hidden here. The corkboard, the outliner and the [inspector](inspector.md) edit them.

## Moving around

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move through the text, and on into the next note and back |
| Page Up, Page Down | Move a screen at a time |
| Ctrl+Home, Ctrl+End (Cmd on macOS) | Go to the start or end of the whole manuscript |

The [contents](inspector.md#the-contents) in the sidebar list the whole book. Click a row there and the manuscript
scrolls to that section.

**Go to previous scene** and **Go to next scene** (commands) move through the binder in its order.

## Titles and headings

- **Click a note's title**, or press F2 in the note, to rename it.
- **Ctrl-click** the title (Cmd-click on macOS) to open the note in its own tab.
- **Right-click** the title for the menu its card has: status, label, **New note after this**, move up or down,
  delete, snapshots. See [a card's menu](corkboard.md#a-cards-menu).
- **Click a folder's heading** to open that folder in the view.

## Commands that work here

Because each section is the note's own editor, commands for the open note apply to the section the cursor is in:

- **Split scene at cursor** and **Split scene with selection as title**. See
  [Splitting, merging and grouping](splitting-and-merging.md).
- **Take a snapshot**, **Rewrite** and **Show snapshots**. See [Snapshots](snapshots.md).
- **Toggle focus mode**, and the button at the end of the toolbar. See [Focus mode](focus-mode.md).

## Long binders

Long binders stay quick. Only the sections near the screen are live editors; the rest show as rendered text until
you scroll to them.

Rendered text stands as its editor will, line for line, so the page doesn't move when you click into a section.
Footnotes are shown there as the editor shows them: each one's text where you wrote it, small, with `[^1]` in the
line, not as a list at the foot.

## On a phone

On a phone a section is rendered text until you tap it. The tap puts the caret on the letter under your finger and
opens the keyboard, and the section is its editor for as long as you're in it. Tap another section and the first
goes back to rendered text once what you typed is saved. On a tablet, as on a computer, the sections near the screen
are editors already. See [Phones and tablets](mobile.md).

## Limits

- If an Obsidian update changes the editor Binders uses here, the manuscript falls back to read only and says so.
  Click a section to open its note and edit it there. See [Compatibility](compatibility.md).
- A binder made by a newer version of Binders is read only in every mode.
